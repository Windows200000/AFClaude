Its inaccuracy and the ratio's variance are computed in code, not tuned by hand: pacing.forecast_backtest() back-calculates the forecast error at every past night decision point, pacing.threshold_info() reports bias / sd / rmse, the ratio's spread and the full-session cost's uncertainty (the UI shows them next to the threshold), and data/forecast_log.jsonl + pacing.forecast_errors() score the logged live decisions. Making that error smaller (lower sd, bias near 0, fewer under-predictions) is your lever; there is no margin or target to hand-tune.
